About the project

Information from the community, for the community.

Jijiga Electricity is a community information project designed to make recent electricity observations easier to understand.

Instead of relying on a single person's observation, multiple community reports can provide a broader picture of electricity conditions across different Kebeles.

Community information may help residents understand local conditions and may provide useful signals for electricity service stakeholders. Reports on this website are community observations and are not official service records.
Trust & transparency

Clear information. Honest reporting.

The goal is to provide useful community information without pretending that community reports are official electricity service records.

Community observations

Reports come from people observing electricity conditions in their local area.

Recent information

The dashboard focuses on recent reports so that older information does not dominate the current picture.

Independent project

Jijiga Electricity is an independent community information initiative and is not an official EEU reporting platform.

Contact & community

Help make local electricity information better.

Community participation is what makes this project useful. When people report honestly and responsibly, the information can become more meaningful for the wider community and electricity service stakeholders.
