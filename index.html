About the project

A simple idea for a more informed community.

Jijiga Electricity is a community-driven information platform designed to make local electricity conditions easier to understand.

Instead of relying on one person's experience, residents can share what they are seeing in their own Kebele. When many recent observations are available, the community can get a clearer picture of where electricity is available, unavailable, or unstable.

  • ✓ Community-based information
  • ✓ Recent reports prioritized
  • ✓ Simple reporting for residents
  • ✓ Public and transparent information

🌍 Community impact

Your report can become useful information.

When residents consistently report what they observe, the collected information can help reveal patterns across Jijiga.

For example, if many recent reports indicate that a particular Kebele is without electricity, that information may help residents understand the situation and may also provide useful community-level information to electricity service stakeholders.

Trust & transparency

Information should be useful and honest.

We want people to understand exactly what this platform does — and what it does not do.

👥

Community submitted

Electricity conditions are based on reports submitted by people observing their local area.

🕐

Recent information matters

Recent reports receive priority when showing the current community picture.

🔐

Repeat reporting is limited

The platform uses local protection to reduce repeated submissions from the same device.

🔄

Shared information

Reports are stored in a shared database so the community can see recent activity.

⚖️

Not an official outage system

This website does not replace official electricity announcements or emergency reporting channels.
